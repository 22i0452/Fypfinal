Protection, with a receipt.

Access & consent

Signed-in, assigned-doctor access. Required consent before capture. Reports and recordings stay owned.

Medicine wording

Keep source names and linked dose/avoid instructions. Changed wording invalidates review; unresolved names require doctor review.

Structure & evidence

Validate model JSON and supplied source IDs. Model output cannot grant doctor approval. Draft finalization remains blocked.

State & recovery

Reject stale selections and source versions. Preserve checkpoints, explicit fallback state and immutable saved versions.

Payload minimization

Test direct identifier removal while retaining clinical meaning. Pattern checks do not prove complete de-identification.

Bounded evaluation

Isolated clinic, fixed cases, request budget and worker deadline. Synthetic runs block outbound network; replay uses saved receipts.

What remains untested.

Authored scenarios and uploader-attested recordings are a small evaluation pack, not an independently annotated clinical dataset. Development and held-out clips run separately; once used for tuning, a held-out recording is no longer an untouched test.

Live text has no audio input. Recorded audio does not test browser microphone capture, telephone transport, overlapping speech or timed acoustic speaker clustering. A reference link establishes provenance, not medical correctness.

Stopped, interrupted and unexecuted cases stay visible. No percentages appear without a denominator. No claim of 100% clinical accuracy. Your latest 50 reports are retained; up to 20 recordings can be saved.
