What this report establishes

Synthetic runs use authored inputs and controlled provider responses against the real booking, persistence, revision and authorization logic. The report records application checks, not clinical accuracy. Live text checks use the real provider and a small factual checklist, without speech input.

Stopped, interrupted and unexecuted scenarios remain labelled. No results are generated until a run actually executes. Reports belong to your signed-in account; the most recent 50 are retained.
